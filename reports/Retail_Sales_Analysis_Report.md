# Retail Sales Analysis Report

## 1. Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze retail sales data using Microsoft Excel and MySQL to identify sales trends, customer behavior, and product performance.

## 2. Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- MySQL
- SQL

## 3. Key Performance Indicators
- **Total Sales:** 2,261,526.40
- **Total Orders:** 4,922
- **Total Customers:** 793

## 4. Key Findings
- **Regional performance:** The West region recorded the highest sales, while the South region recorded the lowest sales.
- **Product performance:** Chairs and Phones were among the highest-selling sub-categories.
- **Customer behavior:** The Consumer segment contributed the highest sales, based on the initial analysis.
- **Product opportunity:** Lower-selling sub-categories may need further investigation to understand customer demand.

## 5. Recommendations
- Investigate why sales are lower in the South region.
- Review product demand and sales trends by sub-category.
- Analyze monthly sales to identify seasonal patterns.
- Include profit and discount analysis if those fields are available in the dataset.

## 6. Conclusion
This project demonstrates the use of Excel and SQL to summarize retail sales, compare business performance, and identify areas for further investigation.
